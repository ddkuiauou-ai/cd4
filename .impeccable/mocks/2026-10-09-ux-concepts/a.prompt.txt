Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a polished Korean mobile-web UX concept storyboard for the existing stock-information service "천하제일 단타대회". This is a realistic product-interface image for design review, not a promotional poster. The intended experience is: arrive to check one number, enjoy the rankings, become curious about another stock, keep browsing.
Output one wide landscape board, approximately 2400 by 1700 pixels. Show THREE large, equally sized tall mobile-web screen panels side by side in left-to-right reading order. Each panel has about a 390x844 viewport ratio, scaled up for excellent readability. No photographed phone bodies, no perspective, no desktop monitor, no extra miniature screens. Keep an elegant shallow outer margin, show full unclipped screens. Above the panels put the board's concept title in readable Korean. Beneath the panels use only the concise captions "순위 발견", "종목 확인", "이어서 탐색", joined by subtle arrows. This board is a journey storyboard, and every screen must feel like a usable page in the SAME product. Korean text should be clean, verbatim, and legible; prefer fewer bigger labels over fine print.
Shared brand: show the full existing name "천하제일 단타대회" in the header of each screen with expressive Korean Myeongjo ExtraBold lettering, "천하제일" vermilion and "단타대회" golden yellow, a small yellow circular emblem with a red five-point star between the words. Retain the name and nostalgic tournament warmth, express through original type and graphic rhythm. Use modern clear Korean sans-serif for data. Body text dark and easy to read. All screens display the readable label "예시 데이터". Every financial number is deliberately synthetic sample content, never present it as current market data. Keep the SAME scope throughout: individual listed common stocks, not combined corporations.
SCREEN 1, rankings: compact brand header, useful search field labeled "종목 검색", metric navigation "시가총액", "PER", "배당". Title "종목 시가총액". Show a readable coherent ordered ranking list with headings "순위", "종목", "시가총액". Exact rows: "1 삼성전자 495조 유지"; "2 SK하이닉스 237조 ↑1"; "3 LG에너지솔루션 88조 ↓1"; "4 삼성바이오로직스 77조 ↑1"; "5 현대차 55조 ↓1". Visually select the SK하이닉스 row, with a clear detail-opening affordance. Rank and rank movement are grouped; do not add stock-price percentages to the ranking list. Give top ranks genuine color and identity without making all rows separate floating cards. End with a small understated "전체 순위 CSV" utility link, lower in hierarchy than stock exploration. Real list content should occupy most of the viewport, not a large hero.
SCREEN 2, selected stock: compact same brand header and back link "시가총액 순위"; title "SK하이닉스", secondary "보통주 · 예시 데이터". Immediately and clearly answer "시가총액 237조" and "2위". Show "이전 3위 → 현재 2위" as rank movement, separated from the stock-price line "주가 326,000원" with "+1.20%" in Korean-market up red. Beneath the chart, a visibly useful affordance "가까운 순위 보기" and a short teaser of nearby stocks. Keep one clear reading order; no repeated grids of the same current number.
SCREEN 3, continue exploring: same brand header, back link "SK하이닉스"; title "가까운 시가총액 순위", readable "예시 데이터". Show connected neighboring ranks, not a bracket: "1위 삼성전자" with "495조" and "258조 더 큼"; current position "2위 SK하이닉스" with "237조"; "3위 LG에너지솔루션" with "88조" and "149조 더 작음". Make relationships intuitively clear and neighboring entries clearly tappable. Show the LG에너지솔루션 entry activated with a compact anchored quick-preview surface beneath or alongside it, as a plausible tap state that can also be a desktop hover. Preview repeats only "LG에너지솔루션", "88조 · 3위" and action "종목 보기". Do not obscure the main comparison. Hint that ranking context is retained when moving onward.
Interaction intent should read visually through selected state, coherent navigation, and that single preview. No fingers, cursor giant graphics, animation trails, gratuitous confetti, star ratings, trading controls, portfolios, signup, prize pools, tournament participation, buy/sell recommendations, fake market intelligence, claims of real-time data, real brands' logos, literal anime characters, or copyrighted scenes. No purple SaaS gradients, glassmorphism, excessive rounded KPI tiles, black neon crypto dashboard, or grey admin dashboard. Flat readable interfaces with intentional color, crisp alignment, realistic touch targets and restrained functional depth.
CONCEPT A: board title "A · 대회 관전판".
Art direction: exuberant but well-crafted late-1990s tournament scoreboard and sports result graphics translated into a bright Korean mobile product. Warm white main reading surfaces, richly saturated golden-yellow masthead accents (#F8AD02), vermilion (#DC3030), ink-dark structural rules, a limited deep blue secondary accent. Bold distinctive compact title bands, large colored rank numbers or small solid rank plates for the top three, stable open table rows. Let the gold and red have enough area to make the service memorable, not just tiny decoration. A compact asymmetric score-strip rhythm connects current rank and one-step movement. Selected SK하이닉스 has an unmistakable gold-tinted row and firm readable typography. The detail feels like focusing on one contender's record; the large "2위" is integrated with its identity and 237조 without overpowering the actual number. Nearby positions on screen three form a vertical ranking rail with a clear highlighted current position. The anchored preview is confident, bright, and crisp. Use small original star geometry from the existing emblem as a recurring navigation detail, sparingly. A mature nostalgic sense of play for adults: purposeful graphic energy, not a children's app and not a game battle screen. All three screens share the same typographic and color rules. Design each screen around its own user question, not one reusable dashboard template.
